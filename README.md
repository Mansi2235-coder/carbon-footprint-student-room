# Carbon Footprint of My Student Room

An Excel estimate of the yearly CO2 emissions from the electricity I use in my room in a student residence in Munich.

## Method
- Electricity use (kWh per year) = power (kW) x hours per week x 52.
- Emissions (kg CO2) = kWh x 0.363 kg CO2/kWh.
- Emission factor: German electricity mix 2024, 363 g CO2/kWh ([Umweltbundesamt](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/themen/co2-emissionen-pro-kilowattstunde-strom-2024)).
- Included: laptop at desk, light, hair dryer, phone charger. Hours come from my own weekly routine; power values are typical rated values, not measured.

## Result (estimate)
- About **177 kWh** of electricity per year, which is about **64 kg CO2 per year**.
- The laptop is the largest source (about 55%), followed by the hair dryer (about 26%) and the light (about 14%).

## Not included
- Heating (included in rent, no data), router (flat fee, shared), fridge (shared kitchen), and cleaning and shared areas (done by the residence).

## Limits
- CO2 only, not CO2-equivalent. This is an estimate for one person and one room, not a measurement.
